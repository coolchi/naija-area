# Abule (10 Oct 2026)

**What it is:** a small, light village in Ogun State, reached by road from Ijebu Ode (fare 300, ESTIMATE, through the ledger). Fictional and generic: Sherif said to use the name "Abule" as-is and not to worry about the real place-name concern. Generated by `tools/gen-abule.mjs` into `server/config/cities/ogun-abule.json`.

**Everything is invented (layout, prices, notices) and NOT verified. Someone who knows village life in Ogun should review it.**

## What is in it (300 x 230 m)
- **Huts and low houses:** 14 round mud huts with thatch (decor, solid) and 6 low houses for rent at about 40 percent of the usual price (the cheapest housing in the game; a test compares it with every other city).
- **Market Square:** a paved square, five kiosks, a market stall shop and the market trader job.
- **Village Hall (venue):** a notice board, benches for Whot, Ayo and the word board, house rules. The notice text is a placeholder.
- **Mama's Kitchen (venue):** pounded yam, egusi, moin moin, zobo (game prices) and the usual game benches. **Abule Provisions** (shop) and a **Health Post** (clinic).
- **Well, stream and footbridge:** a stone well with a roof; a stream (8 m wide) that cannot be waded: the only crossings are the Market Road gap and a short footbridge, and a test checks the gap matches the road width.
- **Farm and Farm Hand job:** 8 crop rows on tilled soil with a scarecrow, walkable, and a new job (`farm`, 90 s shift, wage 1500, no unlock shifts) at the farm gate. A goat pen with six goats next to it.
- **Chapel:** a small plain gabled chapel with a bell tower and a cross, with a sacred buffer (no billboard within 45 m; a test checks it). Shown respectfully; the design needs local review.
- **Palm-lined road:** 46 palms along Market Road, skipping the stream.
- **Roads:** Market Road, Well Road and a curved Farm Lane. One NPC traffic loop. 3 billboards.
- **Backdrop:** low green hills instead of the Lagos towers (a new `city.skyline` option: `towers`, `lowrise`, `hills`; Kano uses lowrise, the Ogun towns and Abule use hills).

## NOT done / NOT verified
- **Market day** (a weekly busy market with extra stalls) is not built: the shops have no day-of-week rule yet.
- No festival, wrestling or football event, no livestock you can buy, no farming minigame beyond the timed shift, no village chief or elders.
- No local-flavour text in toasts and chat (needs a contributor), no mosque or shrine.
- NPC traffic and pedestrians are the shared mix; a village would have far fewer cars (the loop exists but is not tuned).
- Rents, wage and the fare are estimates. Phone frame rate: measured only in software rendering (see the smoke output).
