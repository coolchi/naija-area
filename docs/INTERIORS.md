# Interiors: design note

Everything inside a building is generated from **config data + small primitives** (boxes, cylinders, spheres) in the low-poly style. No external assets. All sizes, prices, fees and moods are **placeholders (ESTIMATE)**.

## Houses (per type)
* Data: `server/config/balance.js → housing.types`. Each property in the city JSON has a `style` (set by `tools/gen-city.mjs`); `server/core/housing.js` resolves it (`typeOf`, `gridOf`, `maxGuestsOf`, `reservedRects`, `layoutOf`).
* Six types: `faceyou` (face-me-I-face-you room, 6x5, shared corridor), `selfcon` (self-contain studio 6x7 with ensuite and kitchenette), `flat2` (2-bedroom flat 8x8), `bungalow` (9x8 with veranda), `duplex` (10x10 with stairs and landing), `mansion` (12x12 with grand stairs, pillars, bar, chandelier). Guest caps 2/2/4/5/6/8.
* Each type defines: grid, door position, windows (wall + position + width), built-in fixtures, default wall/floor palette ids, guest cap. Fixtures with `blocks:true` are **reserved cells**: Buy mode cannot place furniture there. The doorway (2 cells in front of the door) is always reserved. **The server enforces this** (`BLOCKED`, `OUT_OF_ROOM`); the client only mirrors it for the green/red ghost.
* Client: `client/src/rooms.js → houseShell()` draws windows, door frame (open leaf), corridor/veranda and the fixtures; `interior.js` keeps Buy mode. Works for the owner (Buy mode, rename, paint) and for accepted visitors (read-only + guest actions), opened from the house card or the map.
* Land plots: a bought plot shows a **staged build look** (`housing.plotStages`: pegged and fenced → foundation → blockwork → roofed; ESTIMATE days). It is a status on the plot card/map; plots are not enterable.

## Venues (every venue has its own room)
City JSON `venues[].room` = `{ w, d, wall, trim, floor{style,a,b}, windows[], mood, decor[] }`, generated by `tools/gen-city.mjs`. `rooms.js → venueShell()` + `decorate()` build floor (checker/planks/plain/dance), walls, trim, windows (big/blind), mood lighting and the decor kinds (shelf, fridge, stools, lanterns, fan, benches, speakers, pennants, neon, hanging lamps, lift, tool wall, tyres, drums, turntables, spotlights, stage, light rig, disco ball, back bar, VIP sofas and rope, mirrors, dryers, barber pole ...). Animations (fan, disco ball, light rig, dance tiles, record decks, barber pole) run from a per-view `anim` list.

## Clubs and salons (server)
`server/core/leisure.js`, config `balance.leisure`. Club: dance (mood, cooldown), bar (soft drinks only, fee via ledger), VIP corner (fee via ledger, cooldown), song board (placeholder). Salon: pick a style at a chair (`GET /api/salon`, `POST /api/salon/style`), fee through the ledger (`sys:sink_fees`), small mood lift, a "fresh" look for 2 h shown on the profile (`hair`), shared 10-minute cooldown; the wash basin gives a tiny mood lift. Cooldowns are in memory (reset on server restart; ESTIMATE, fine for placeholders).

## Garage
`client/src/garage-view.js`: phone → Garage → **Open my garage**. Three painted slots (`balance.cars.garageSlots`), owned cars standing in them, the active car ringed in green, orbit camera, pick a car to see condition/value and **Use this car**. Empty slots say "Empty slot". The Auto Mart and Meco have their own rooms (see table).

## Tests / screenshots
`tests/interiors.test.js` (types, grids, reserved cells, guest caps, buy-mode refusals, plot stages, venue rooms, clubs, salons). `npm run smoke:interiors` opens every house type, every venue, both clubs, both salons and the garage in headless Chrome and saves `screenshots/interior-<type>.png`, `interior-venue-*.png`, `club-*.png`, `salon-*.png`, `garage-own.png`.

## Detail pass (10 Oct 2026, approved by Sherif: "everything just look box, box, box")
Target look: `docs/interior-reference.png` (round table with red chairs, teal sofa with yellow cushions, striped rug, bed with pillows and blanket, wardrobe, wall TV, ceiling fan, louvre windows, flag, wall clock, plant, water dispenser).
- **`client/src/props.js`** is the prop library (about 55 pieces): chairs with backs and legs, stools, sofas with puffy cushions and throw pillows, round dining set with four chairs, beds (headboard, mattress, striped blanket, pillows), wardrobes with panelled doors and cornice, bookshelves with books/jars/bottles, TV units, fridges, cookers with gas cylinder, kitchen counters with sink, water dispenser, generator, plants (leafy and snake plant), floor and table lamps, Ankara-style striped rug with fringe, curtains (open or closed), wall art, Nigerian flag, wall clock, mirrors with bulbs, ceiling fan (spins), louvre windows, AC unit, speakers, DJ booth, bar and back bar with bottles, bar stools, salon chair, wash basin, hair dryer, tool wall, workbench, tyre stack, oil drum, tool cart, pillar, stairs with rail, bathroom (tiles, toilet, basin, shower curtain), food counter, game table, chandelier, partition screen.
- **How it stays cheap:** each prop is ONE merged geometry with baked vertex colours (plus one small unlit geometry for glowing bits such as screens and lamp shades), cached per kind/size/look, with two shared materials. Rooms are built only when entered. Walls and floors use small shared 128 px canvas textures (stucco, tile, planks) tinted by the paint colour, so Paint still works. No external assets.
- **Config driven:** house fixtures and Buy-mode items come from `balance.housing`; venue decor lists live in the city JSON (`tools/add-room-detail.mjs` added wall decor, waiting corners and ambient crowds, idempotent, `room.detailV2`). New decor kinds in the JSON: `flag clock art ac window curtain foodCounter gameTable chair coffeeTable floorLamp tv waterDispenser toolCart workbench generator crowd`, plus the older ones. Walls get flag/clock/art on free space only (windows and fixtures decide).
- **People in rooms:** clubs have 9 dancers, the eatery, viewing centre and joint have small groups, all drawn with the same `Crowd` human shape as street walkers (7 draw calls for any number of people).
- **Server rules unchanged:** sizes, prices, blocked cells and guest caps are untouched. Right-wall windows are not drawn (that wall is cut away). Side fixtures face +x so their fronts face the camera.
- **Budget (placeholder ESTIMATES in `server/config/perf-budget.js`):** at most 160 draw calls and 40,000 triangles per open room; one prop at most 3,500 triangles; a catalogue of one of each at most 60,000. Measured on this box (software GL, not a phone): houses 32 to 61 draw calls and 3,000 to 10,000 triangles; venues 57 to 115 calls and 5,600 to 12,800 triangles; garage 67 calls and 9,600 triangles. Real-phone frame rate is NOT measured.
- **Tools:** `node scripts/props-preview.mjs` renders contact sheets of all props (`screenshots/props-sheet-N.png`).
