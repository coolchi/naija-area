# Kano (10 Oct 2026)

**What it is:** the second city and second air hub. You fly Lagos to Kano (cross-state fare) or back from the airport terminal; Kano is its own city with its own presence, chat, ads, shops, jobs and venues (the multi-city rules apply unchanged). Generated by `tools/gen-kano.mjs` into `server/config/cities/kano.json`, registered in `server/config/cities/index.json`.

**Everything here is a FICTIONAL layout with real place names taken from `17_cities_and_airport_plan.md`. Positions, sizes, dishes, prices and the little Hausa text are NOT verified. A contributor from Kano must review it before any public launch** (see "Content care").

## What is in it (480 x 405 m, no water)
- **Roads:** three north-south (Kurmi Road, Palace Road, Sabon Gari Road), three east-west (Airport Link Road, Market Road, Dala Road), plus a curved Old City Ring. Traffic loops come from the shared district kit.
- **Kurmi-style market:** a block of market buildings with a market stall shop and a market job.
- **Palace Square (generic):** a paved square. It is a plain public square; the real palace is NOT depicted.
- **Old City wall (generic):** mud-brick wall with battlements and gate towers along the south and west edges, open at every road. Visual only (it stands just outside the walkable edge).
- **Dye pits (generic):** a yard of 14 round pits with coloured liquid and drying cloth lines, plus the **Dye pit works** venue (a poster about dyeing, the Word of the Day board, house rules). The poster text is a placeholder.
- **Dala Hill:** scenery hill seen over the northern edge. You cannot climb it.
- **Kano Canteen:** tuwo shinkafa with miyan taushe, suya, masa and kunu (game prices, ESTIMATE), with the normal Whot, Ayo and word-board stations.
- **Kano Mart, a clinic, a POS kiosk, 15 homes (rent), 3 jobs, 7 billboards** (the ad inventory for Kano; ads are per city).
- **Airport:** terminal at (183, -198), runway and apron outside the north-east edge, same shape as Lagos. Flights use the existing flight system; nothing was changed in `flight.js`.

## Content care (the rules this city follows)
1. **No clubs, bars, DJ or dance stations, VIP lists, betting or alcohol anywhere.** `city.contentRules` says so and a test (`tests/kano.test.js`) scans every venue, shop item, landmark and building label for those words.
2. **Ads:** campaigns for Kano that match a restricted category (bet, casino, lottery, alcohol and so on, the list in `ratecard.js`) are **auto-rejected** (`city.contentRules.rejectRestrictedAds`). In other cities the same ads are only flagged for human review, as before.
3. **Hausa text:** a single placeholder greeting ("Sannu da zuwa") in the canteen blurb, labelled as a placeholder in the text itself. A test makes sure any Hausa text says "placeholder". Do not add more without a Hausa speaker.
4. **No real buildings are copied.** The palace, the central mosque and Kofar Mata dye pits are not reproduced; the dye pit yard, the square and the wall are generic shapes.

## NOT done / NOT verified
- Real Kano layout, distances and street names (invented). Dish names and prices (estimate).
- Whether "Dala Hill" and "Sabon Gari" should appear as names in a game; a local contributor decides.
- No Hausa translation of the interface. No call to prayer sounds, on purpose, until someone from Kano says how (and whether) to do it.
- No mosque or other worship building yet (the Lagos ones were approved by name; Kano's were not).
- No Kano-specific jobs (a dye or textile job would need a new job definition and balance numbers).
- No road link or train from Lagos: Kano is flight-only.
